## 🎮 About the Game

Fell & Sell is a single-player first-person mod-and-slash roguelike with shop-management and simulation elements. It was released on August 26, 2026, and was developed by Art Games Studio S.A.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Fell &amp; Sell**. This trainer enhances the core dungeon-diving, crafting, inventory, combat, and shop-management systems in Fell & Sell. It helps players survive deeper expeditions, protect valuable loot, accelerate crafting, and build a profitable fantasy store.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to explore deeper dungeon floors, experiment with altar effects, collect rare materials, craft equipment freely, protect expedition loot, or expand their shop without repeating resource-grinding sections.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### ⚔️ Combat

- **God Mode** — Prevents the player from losing health during dungeon and forest encounters.
- **Unlimited Stamina** — Removes stamina restrictions for repeated attacks, blocking, and movement.
- **Unlimited Mana** — Keeps mana available for abilities and magical effects.
- **One-Hit Kills** — Allows the player to defeat skeletons and other dungeon enemies with a single successful attack.

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **No Loot Loss on Death** — Preserves the unbanked loot collected during an expedition if the player dies.
- **Movement Speed** — Adjusts walking and running speed for faster dungeon traversal and forest exploration.
- **Fast Quest Rewards** — Improves the pace of rewards earned through completed Quest Board objectives.

### 🧰 Inventory

- **Unlimited Inventory Capacity** — Prevents inventory overload while collecting weapons, armor, materials, recipes, and valuables.
- **Inventory Item Multiplier** — Increases the quantity of selected materials and items collected during runs.

### 💰 Economy

- **Unlimited Gold** — Provides a stable supply of gold for purchases, shop growth, and other expenses.
- **Shop Sales Multiplier** — Boosts earnings from selling dungeon loot and crafted goods.
- **Maximum Shop Prestige** — Raises shop prestige to improve the effectiveness of the store-management loop.

### 📌 Crafting

- **Free Crafting** — Removes material costs when forging weapons, crafting armor, and preparing useful items.
- **Instant Crafting** — Completes available crafting actions immediately.

### 📌 Exploration

- **Dungeon Loot Multiplier** — Increases the amount of loot obtained from dungeon expeditions without changing the dungeon structure.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Fell & Sell uses first-person movement across the shop, dungeon floors, and the forest. Travel through the dungeon carefully, open routes as you advance, and use the movement option when you want to move between rooms or return to safety faster. The speed setting is especially useful during long expeditions and forest-foraging trips.

### Tools & Inventory

Dungeon runs provide weapons, armor, materials, recipes, and other items for crafting or sale. Inventory space is limited during normal play, and an overloaded inventory can restrict what you carry. Use the inventory-capacity option before collecting large hauls, then return to the shop or cabin to organize goods, craft equipment, and prepare items for display.

### World & Environment

The main gameplay loop connects the shop, descending roguelike dungeons, and the living forest. Deeper dungeon floors offer rarer loot but increase the risk of losing an unbanked haul on death. Forest expeditions provide additional materials and introduce events such as goblin attacks, thunderstorms, and mushroom-season effects. Altars found during runs can significantly alter the player's build and combat style.

### Combat & Weapons

Combat is built around first-person mod-and-slash encounters against skeletons and other monsters. Manage health, stamina, mana, weapon condition, and positioning while advancing through dungeon rooms. Craft stronger weapons and tougher armor between expeditions, use altar effects to change your playstyle, and activate defensive or offensive trainer options only after the game has finished loading into an active area.
